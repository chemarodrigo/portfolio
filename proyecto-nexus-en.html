Tech stack

01

HTML5

Four documents sharing a header and footer. The form uses fieldset, legend and associated labels, with the active state marked by aria-current.

02

CSS Grid

A single stylesheet for the whole site. Each section sets its proportions with fractions, and the cards lift and turn lime on hover.

03

Google Fonts

Syne at weight 800 for the headlines and the logo, and Space Grotesk for body text, forms and the pricing table.

04

Font Awesome

Iconography for the project covers, the agency values and the link arrows, always hidden from assistive technology.

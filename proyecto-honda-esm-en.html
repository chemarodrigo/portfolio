Project 07 · Frontend development

Honda Digital Workshop Manual

Digitising the official Honda ESM service manual (Accord 1998), turning obsolete Windows-only software into a modern progressive web app that is responsive and reachable from any device

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Adobe Creative Cloud Responsive Mobile-First

Context

The problem

Honda's official electronic service manual (Honda ESM v1.4.0) was distributed as Windows-only software using proprietary .CAB files that cannot be extracted with conventional tools. Over the years the software has become obsolete and incompatible with current operating systems, leaving a technical resource that is essential for maintaining the vehicle almost entirely out of reach. No alternative digital version offers this content in a modern, accessible form.

The goals

Recover all the technical content of the original Honda ESM (more than 100 technical diagrams and images, including engine diagrams, wiring schematics and body drawings) and turn it into a progressive web app that can be installed on any device, works offline and offers a modern, responsive browsing experience. The end goal is to have the workshop manual always available on a phone, even in the garage with no internet connection.

The audience

Primarily personal use for maintaining my own car, but potentially useful to the Honda Accord Type R owners' community as well: a small but passionate group of enthusiasts who value originality and the correct maintenance of these increasingly rare cars.

Approach

Research

The starting point was the original Honda ESM v1.4.0 software, a Windows-only program that stores all its content in proprietary files impossible to extract with conventional tools. That meant the only route to recovering the content was an entirely manual process: capturing the original software's screens one by one, cataloguing every image, documenting every section and mapping the whole navigation structure. I analysed the program's internal folders, identifying and extracting more than 100 technical images so far across engine diagrams, wiring schematics and body drawings, although the original manual contains hundreds more that will be added progressively.

Design decisions

The main challenge was faithfully recreating a very complex information architecture, built entirely by hand. The original manual is organised into 16 main sections (from general information, specifications and maintenance through to engine, brakes, suspension, body, electrical system and wiring diagrams), which in turn break down into more than 200 subsections with their own detail pages. That whole hierarchy was mapped and organised manually to replicate the navigation experience of the original software. Visually, Honda's corporate colours (red and black) were adopted to keep the brand identity, and the interface was designed with navigation cards, breadcrumbs and a search system that makes it quick to locate any procedure within such an extensive structure.

Technical challenges

The biggest challenge was the scale of the manual work: in the current phase alone, more than 100 technical images tied to over 200 subsections have been catalogued, captured and organised, and the original manual holds hundreds more still to digitise. Any error assigning an image to its section could lead a mechanic to follow the wrong procedure, so the verification process was exhaustive. Making an application with this volume of content (which will keep growing) run smoothly on a phone, even with no internet connection inside a garage, required careful optimisation of image loading and section navigation. The project remains in active development, progressively expanding the sections with complete content.

Tech stack

01

HTML5 / CSS3

The base languages for building the application's structure and visual appearance, letting it adapt to any screen size so the technical diagrams read correctly on both a phone and a computer.

02

JavaScript

Responsible for all of the app's interactivity: navigation between sections, the search system, progressive image loading and the ability to use the manual without an internet connection.

03

Adobe Creative Cloud

Tools such as Photoshop were used to process and optimise the manual's images, reducing their weight so the app loads quickly on mobile devices without losing the quality needed to read the technical diagrams correctly.

04

Responsive Design

An adaptive layout that allows engine diagrams, wiring schematics and body drawings to be consulted from a phone in the garage or on a large screen to study more complex procedures in detail.

Tech stack

01

HTML5

Every block uses its proper element: header, section, article, figure and footer, with a skip link and ARIA labels for screen readers.

02

CSS Grid

A custom stylesheet, no frameworks. Each section defines its proportions with fractions, and a sticky header follows the scroll.

03

Google Fonts

Playfair Display for the headlines and DM Sans for body text: a contrast between an editorial serif and a neutral, comfortable sans.

04

Font Awesome

Iconography for music genres, studio services and social links, always hidden from assistive technology and paired with text.
